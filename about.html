Ann Marie "Annie" Lawrence, LCMT, is a Board Certified massage therapist (BCTMB #000000-00) and professional member of the American Massage Therapy Association (Member ID #00000). She completed a 900-hour training program at the Muscular Therapy Institute in Cambridge, MA, and holds a Massachusetts massage license (#0000).

Annie has pursued advanced training across a wide range of modalities, including Active Isolated Stretching, Thai Bodywork, Lomi Lomi, and energy-based therapies, allowing her to tailor each session to exactly what a client needs rather than offering one-size-fits-all treatments.

Her mission is to improve quality of life through therapeutic massage and complementary therapies that integrate body, mind, and spirit to enhance overall health and wellbeing.
