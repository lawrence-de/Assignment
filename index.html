Integrative Touch for Body, Mind & Spirit

Annie’s Therapeutic Massage offers personalized massage therapy tailored to each client’s individual needs, serving athletes, students, seniors, and clients of all ages throughout the greater Boston area. Sessions are available both in-home and in a private treatment room, blending techniques to fit exactly what your body needs that day.

Annie's Approach

Annie blends a range of modalities — including Swedish, Deep Tissue, Passive Body, Energy Work, Lomi Lomi, Thai, AIS, and Breath Work — tailored to exactly what each client needs during their session, rather than booked as separate named treatments.
